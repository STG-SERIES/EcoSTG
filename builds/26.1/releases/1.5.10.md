# EcoSTG 1.5.10

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.5.10.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper 1.21.11+, Java 21

## What's updated

### ESC hub
- Removed the extra **Open hub** screen. Tapping EcoSTG on ESC opens the full hub menu again.

### `/sell` → Auction House
- Selling no longer pays you instantly.
- Each item stack is **listed on `/ah`** at its `worth.yml` price (still no typing prices).
- You get paid when a buyer purchases the listing.

## Install

Replace the jar in `plugins/` with `EcoSTG-1.5.10.jar` and **restart**.
