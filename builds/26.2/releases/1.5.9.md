# EcoSTG 1.5.9

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.5.9.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper 1.21.11+, Java 21

## What's updated

### `/sell` — instant sell
- Place items in the sell chest, click the **lime pane** — done.
- Players **do not** set prices. Prices come from `worth.yml`.
- Item tooltips show `~ $ …`; the sell button shows the total.
- Unsellable items (price `0`) are returned to the player.

### `worth.yml`
- On startup, EcoSTG adds a price for **every** Minecraft item Material that is missing.
- Your existing prices are **never** overwritten.
- Price `0` = unsellable (creative-only / blocked items).

### Auction house
- Custom-price AH listings stay available via **List on Auction** in `/shop` (not `/sell`).

## Install

Replace the jar in `plugins/` with `EcoSTG-1.5.9.jar` and **restart**.  
After first start, check `plugins/EcoSTG/worth.yml` — it will grow to include all items.
