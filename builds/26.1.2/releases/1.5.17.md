# EcoSTG 1.5.17

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.5.17.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper or Purpur 1.21.11+, Java 21

## Changes

- Fixed items from **/sell** and the **AH** not stacking with normal copies of the same item.
- Cause: temporary `~ $` sell-GUI lore / PDC was left on stacks when they left the sell menu or were stored/returned from AH.
- Worth decoration is now stripped whenever items return to the player, when listings are created, and when AH items are given back. Already-contaminated stacks are cleaned on contact.

## Upgrade

Replace the jar with `EcoSTG-1.5.17.jar` and **restart**.
Existing inventory items with leftover `~ $` lore are cleaned the next time they pass through sell/AH, or you can drop/pick them after opening/closing `/sell` once (inventory is sanitized on sell GUI close).
