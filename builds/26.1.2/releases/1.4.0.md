# EcoSTG 1.4.0

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.4.0.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper 1.21.11+, Java 21

## What's updated

### Friends menu
- Title shows `X friends / Y following`.
- **Filter** cycles All / Friends / Following / Online.
- **Search** opens a name field with **Cancel** and **Search**.
- People you follow are buttons; click one to open their profile.
- **+ Follow** lists online players you don't follow yet.

### Friend profile
- Player head plus **View Stats**, **Pay**, **Teleport**, **Settings**, and **Unfollow** (or **+ Follow** if you don't follow them yet).
- Pay and TPA require the other player to be online.

### Stats / profile
- Compact preview: head, money (`$ 23.6M` style), kills, deaths, playtime.
- **View Full Profile** adds job, blocks, and mobs (still respects privacy).

### Money nametag
- Shows **only under the player's head in the world**.
- No longer appended to the name in chat, tab, or the console.

### Gamemode (OP)
- Hub **Gamemode** button for operators.
- Survival / Creative / Adventure / Spectator — no `[ON]` prefix.
- Current mode is still shown in the menu body.

## Install

Replace the old jar in `plugins/` with `EcoSTG-1.4.0.jar` and **restart** the server.
