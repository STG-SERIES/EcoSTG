# EcoSTG 1.5.13

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.5.13.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper or Purpur 1.21.11+, Java 21

## Changes

- **Instant /sell payout** — clicking Sell deposits worth.yml money immediately; items stay listed on the AH as prepaid listings (buyers still pay; seller is not paid again).
- **Cancel prepaid listings** — cancelling your own prepaid listing claws back the amount you were paid, then returns the item.
- **Shulker contents** — worth, sell lore, AH price, and `/ah` search include items inside shulker boxes (e.g. a shulker of dirt matches `/ah dirt` and prices box + dirt).
- Docs/plugin metadata note **Paper and Purpur** support (same jar).

## Upgrade

Replace the jar with `EcoSTG-1.5.13.jar` and **restart**.
Existing AH rows get `instant_paid=0` (classic payout on buy). New `/sell` listings are prepaid.
