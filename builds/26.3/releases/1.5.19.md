# EcoSTG 1.5.19

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.5.19.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper or Purpur 1.21.11+, Java 21

## Changes

- Auction House: **no buy confirm** — clicking a listing buys (or cancels your own) immediately.
- After buy/cancel, the AH stays on the **same page** and **same search**.

## Upgrade

Replace the jar with `EcoSTG-1.5.19.jar` and **restart**.
