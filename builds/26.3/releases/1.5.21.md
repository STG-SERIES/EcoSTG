# EcoSTG 1.5.21

**Jar:** `build/libs/EcoSTG-1.5.21.jar`  
**Requires:** Paper or Purpur 1.21.11+, Java 21

## Changes

- Leaderboards: removed duplicate **Back** button when scrolling.
- Night Vision setting is reapplied after **respawn**, **totem of undying**, and whenever NV is cleared while the setting is still on.

## Upgrade

Replace the jar with `EcoSTG-1.5.21.jar` and **restart**.
